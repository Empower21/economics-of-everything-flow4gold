A full house looks like success. But does a bigger crowd always mean a better business? Let us explore a miniature concert, using invented numbers.

At a ticket price of twenty, one hundred and fifty guests attend. Revenue is three thousand. After production, venue and per-person costs, profit is seven hundred and fifty.

Raise the ticket price to thirty. Now one hundred guests attend. Revenue stays at three thousand, but costs fall. Profit rises to one thousand.

Now rent a thousand-place venue without changing interest. The crowd stays the same, while venue costs rise. The event loses twelve hundred and fifty.

More capacity is not the same as more demand. Try your own assumptions. Follow the money, and remember: revenue is not profit.
