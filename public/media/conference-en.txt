What makes a conference worth attending? Money matters, but so does access. This miniature conference lets you explore the trade-off without inventing satisfaction scores.

Five hundred attendees pay forty each. Sponsors contribute ten thousand. Total revenue is thirty thousand. Costs are twenty-five thousand, leaving a five-thousand surplus.

There are only two hundred workshop seats. That gives forty percent of attendees a place. These places show limited access, not how many people want a workshop.

Add one hundred workshop seats. Access rises to sixty percent. Costs increase by one thousand, and surplus falls to four thousand. Both changes are visible.

The choice is about priorities and constraints. Compare two plans, explore catering and shared space, and remember that sponsor contributions alone do not measure sponsor return.
