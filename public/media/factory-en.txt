How can one inexpensive component stop an expensive car? This factory needs one part for every vehicle. There are no other bottlenecks in our simplified model.

The daily target is one hundred vehicles. Starting with one hundred and fifty parts, day one produces one hundred and leaves fifty in stock.

Day two can produce only fifty. On day three the line stops. These are unfilled production opportunities, not automatically lost sales.

Two hundred parts arrive before production on day four. The line makes one hundred vehicles and keeps one hundred parts. Total output is two hundred and fifty.

A second supplier can help if parts arrive in time. Compare its premium with safety stock. Under this example, completed vehicles sell that day. Resilience has costs as well as benefits.
